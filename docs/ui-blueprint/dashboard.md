\# Dashboard Blueprint



\## Purpose



Provide operational awareness and business visibility.



Not a command center.



Not an analytics dashboard.



\---



\# Layout



Top Summary



Middle Operational Section



Bottom Supporting Information



\---



\# Business Summary



Cards:



\- Revenue

\- Expenses

\- Net Margin

\- Margin %



Each card contains:



\- Current value

\- Previous period comparison



\---



\# Attention Needed



Compact counters.



Examples:



Delayed Deliveries



Missing POD



Pending Review



All counters are clickable.



\---



\# Recent Deliveries



Table.



Columns:



\- Delivery ID

\- Client

\- Truck

\- Status

\- Margin

\- POD



5–10 rows.



\---



\# Top Trucks



Ranking list.



Filter:



\- Revenue

\- Margin

\- Margin %

\- Expenses

\- Trips



No charts.



\---



\# Revenue vs Expenses



Single supporting chart.



Small.



Minimal.



Purpose:



Context only.



\---



\# Quick Actions



Buttons:



\- New Delivery

\- Upload POD

\- Generate Report
